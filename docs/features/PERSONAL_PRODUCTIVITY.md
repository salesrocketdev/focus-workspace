# Reading, goals, insights and sound

## Reading
Manual book entry: title, author optional, total pages optional, current page/chapter, cover optional (user supplied or licensed source). Reading activity can link book; sessions record time and optional start/end progress. Validate nonnegative page and upper limit if known. Progress edits may go backwards and must not inflate pages read; distinguish correction from pages read in history. Completion explicit or suggested at final page. No ebook viewer. P1 rich library; core activity reading available P0.

## Goals and insights
Daily focus target configurable per profile; initial proposal 60 min, optional per category later. Use actual active intervals, split by local day, exclude breaks/paused time; preserve session timezone or define report zone consistently. Streak optional and non-punitive; no fake productivity comparison. P0 today total and history; P1 date filters, weekly/monthly trend, category breakdown and CSV/JSON export. Scheduled meeting duration separate from actual focus. No tracking other apps.

## Sound
Optional user-controlled ambient audio using original/licensed assets and independent volume; start/stop and mute. Spotify official embed or open external app first, only if supported in platform and policy. Full control via Spotify Connect/App Remote/Web Playback SDK conditional on account, platform, permissions and quota; no proxy/download/rebroadcast. No obligatory premium or provider account; unsupported state has fallback. Avoid auto-play without gesture. Music and timer lifecycles separate, no forced pause at phase changes.

Acceptance: progress survives restart and corrections; goals match history across midnight; sound failure does not affect timer; disconnect removes tokens; no claims of working Spotify player until tested.
