# V4.40 — Tasks, habits, and capture

- Tasks are reachable from the navigation and dashboard. The same editor handles quick capture and full details. Planned date, deadline, and reserved focus time are independent. Today highlights overdue tasks and supports moving work to tomorrow without changing deadlines.
- Recurrence is anchored to the scheduled occurrence. Completing or skipping keeps the original record and creates the next task with its own ID, so focus history and checklists remain separate. A one-off reschedule preserves the series schedule. Monthly recurrence preserves its calendar day across shorter months.
- Habits have a weekly schedule, a rolling seven-day check-in view, a current streak, and reversible archiving. Habits use the existing local workspace and backup.
- The task inbox can capture Gmail and Outlook email examples and Google Meet and Microsoft Teams invitations. Demo calendar meeting details can create a preparation task. Tasks can also retain a user-supplied HTTPS link.
- The Google Calendar, Outlook Calendar and Spotify connections remain demonstrations. Real calendar sync requires provider application registration, consent, token handling, and integration tests. Calendar availability shown by the demo is fixture data. Task reminders run while the app is open and use the existing notification center.
- On mobile, Today, Tasks and Calendar are directly accessible. Habits, Activities, History and Settings are available under More.
